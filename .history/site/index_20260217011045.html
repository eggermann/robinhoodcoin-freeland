The Mission

🌿

Acquire Freeland

Purchase real land and designate it as self-governed, community-run zones — modern commons where people can live, gather, and create without the barriers of private ownership.

🤝

Support Robin Hood Causes

Allocate funds to charitable and social projects — helping the underprivileged, funding grassroots initiatives, echoing "take from the rich, give to the poor."

🗳️

DAO Governance

RHC holders vote on every decision: which land to buy, which cause to support, how to manage each parcel. True democracy, powered by blockchain.

How It Works

1

Get RHC or a Freeland Stamp

Buy RobinHoodCoin (RHC) for governance power, or a Freeland Stamp NFT to fund a specific land campaign.

2

Vote on Proposals

The community proposes land targets and causes. RHC holders vote. Majority decides.

3

Land Is Acquired

Treasury funds are released via multisig. A legal entity holds the deed on behalf of the DAO.

4

Community Thrives

The land becomes a self-governed community space — open, free, and stewarded by its members.
