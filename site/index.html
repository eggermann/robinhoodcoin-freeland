The Mission

Inspired by the legend of Robin Hood — not as a thief, but as someone who reclaims resources from unjust concentration and returns them to the people.

🌿

Acquire Freeland

Purchase real land and designate it as self-governed, community-run zones — modern commons where people can live, gather, and create without the barriers of private ownership. Each parcel is permanently removed from the speculative market.

🤝

Support Robin Hood Causes

Allocate 20% of funds to charitable and social projects — helping the underprivileged, funding grassroots initiatives, echoing "take from the rich, give to the poor."

🗳️

DAO Governance

RHC holders vote on every decision: which land to buy, which cause to support, how to manage each parcel. True democracy, powered by blockchain. Multisig treasury ensures no single person can misuse funds.

📜 The Charter — Laws of Robin Hood Coin

  1. All funds raised must be used exclusively for buying communal land or supporting Robin Hood-like charitable causes.
  2. Every acquired parcel is permanently removed from the speculative market — it can never be resold for private profit.
  3. Decisions are made democratically by RHC token holders. No single entity controls the treasury.
  4. Full transparency: every contribution, expenditure, and decision is publicly trackable on-chain.
  5. The project operates in the spirit of Robin Hood: redistribute wealth toward the common good.
  6. Each freeland parcel is self-governed by its local community, with strategic oversight by the DAO.
  7. Violence, exclusion, and discrimination have no place in any freeland space.
  8. The AI Soul serves the mission, not individual interests. It propagates collective liberation.

How It Works

1

Get RHC or a Freeland Stamp

Buy RobinHoodCoin (RHC) for governance power, or a Freeland Stamp NFT to fund a specific land campaign. RHC can also be earned by volunteering time or skills.

2

Vote on Proposals

The community proposes land targets and causes. RHC holders vote. Majority decides. Use our Telegram bot: /propose and /vote.

3

Land Is Acquired

Treasury funds are released via multisig. A legal entity (LLC or foundation) holds the deed on behalf of the DAO. Full transparency on-chain.

4

Community Thrives

The land becomes a self-governed community space. Local participants form a council. The AI Soul helps coordinate events, maintenance, and outreach.
