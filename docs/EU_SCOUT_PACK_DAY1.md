# EU Scout Pack — Day 1 (Execution Draft)

## Objective
Build a repeatable EU land-acquisition scouting pipeline aligned with the RobinHoodCoin charter (commons-first, non-speculative, transparent governance).

## Priority Zones
1. Germany: Berlin + Brandenburg belt
2. Switzerland: edge zones near major transit
3. EU low-cost fallback: Portugal/Spain/Italy rural bands

## Acquisition Channels (Start Here)
- Municipal/county auctions and foreclosure listings
- Public land marketplaces with rural/agri filters
- Distressed private listings suitable for non-commercial community use

## Core Filters
- Price band: €8k–€80k initial screen
- Size: 1–20 acres (or urban lot with conversion potential)
- Zoning: rural/agri/community-compatible preferred
- Access: road access + water potential + legal right of way
- Risk flags: protected area conflict, title complexity, utility traps

## Scoring Model (0–100)
- Price feasibility (25)
- Legal/zoning fit with charter (25)
- Infrastructure viability (20)
- Community impact potential (20)
- Execution speed (10)
